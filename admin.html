Store Rules

• Currency: Pakistani Rupees (PKR)
• Standard delivery: PKR 350
• Free delivery when discounted item subtotal reaches PKR 10,000
• Supported categories: Bras, Panties, Sets, Accessories, Body Shaper, Camisole
• Promo codes: Configured securely via Promo Codes tab (1–365 days validity)
• Order stock is reserved at placement; cancelled orders are restocked.
• This starter uses a local SQLite database in data/.
